It’s been a 2011 full of pixel art drawings and animations here at Draw!, well 2012 will be more pixelated than ever!

Today we’re proud to introduce a brand new version of Draw! pixel art editor. This release has a lot of new features you’ll love!

**Users’ profiles  
**You can browse users’ galleries by clicking on user’s name’s link.

**More frames  
**Now you can draw up to 16 frames to make your animations even cooler (if it’s possible). Animations’ frames are also filled with a *preview image* to let you see the final result.

**Flip  
**You can flip frames horizontally and vertically.

**Move  
**You can move frames up and right.

**Rotate**  
You can rotate frames by 90 degrees left.

**Copy and paste**  
Select a frame, click the copy button, then add a new frame or select a different one and paste clipboard into it. Animations are just a few clicks away now!

New Draw! interface is designed by [Francesco Fraioli](http://francescofraioli.com), a lot of things are now *isometric* and the result is a much more clear editor.

We hope you like redesign and new features and we’re waiting for your feedback to help us make Draw! cooler and cooler.

Have fun and keep drawing!
